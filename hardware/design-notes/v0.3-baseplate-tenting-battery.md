# v0.3 Modular Tenting Feet + 18650 Battery Pod (FreeCAD, real STEP geometry)

Status: **conceptual + FreeCAD-scripted, geometrically-verified CAD.**
Produced entirely from the real B-rep solids in
`hardware/cad/v0.3-source/cosmotylleft.step` /
`cosmotylright.step`, using FreeCAD 1.1.3's Python API
(`freecadcmd.exe`, `Part` module) for STEP parsing and boolean CSG — not
OpenSCAD mesh-projection workarounds. This supersedes v0.2's flat-tray
retrofit, which worked around the v1 case's non-manifold STL mesh; the
STEP files have no such problem.

New source files used: `cosmotylleft.step` / `cosmotylright.step` (the
regenerated, higher-tenting Cosmos export the user provided). Scripts:
`hardware/cad/v0.3-source/_register_analysis.py` (read-only geometry
inspection, reusable) and `hardware/cad/v0.3/build_v03.py` (the actual
generator, re-derives everything below from the STEP files itself — no
hardcoded coordinates from a prior run).

## Design decision: feet + separate battery pod, not a full-footprint wedge

A literal full-footprint tilted slab under the whole ~150–167mm-wide plate
would need `width × tan(angle)` of extra material at the tall edge — at
20° that's ~54–61mm of solid plastic across the entire board, which is
impractical (weight, print time, and it re-introduces the "doesn't really
need to be full width" problem). Instead, v0.3 uses:

1. **3 discrete tenting feet**, each a small (15×15mm) post that reuses 3
   of the 7 verified case↔plate screw positions (chosen by brute-force
   maximizing the triangle area they form, for a stable, wobble-free
   3-point stance — 3 points always make full contact even if the desk or
   case has minor warp, unlike 4). Each foot is just a rectangular block
   of a **different height**; the tenting angle is encoded entirely in the
   height differences, not in each foot's own geometry. A matching M3
   clearance hole (r=1.75mm) runs through each foot so the **same existing
   screw** passes foot → plate hole → case boss.
2. **A separate 18650 battery pod**, its own part with 2 new self-tapping
   mount points into the plate (not reusing the tenting-foot holes),
   placed near the plate's inner (thumb-side) edge where tenting gives the
   most vertical clearance.

This is the "stacked/modular shim" mechanism from the task's suggested
options, but anchored to real, verified screw positions instead of guessed
corner points (the failure mode flagged in the v0.1/v0.2 notes).

## Tenting mechanism and angle range

- **Mechanism:** interchangeable foot-height sets. `build_v03.py` is fully
  parametric on `TENT_ANGLES_DEG`; regenerating a new angle is a one-line
  change, not a redesign.
- **Range generated this pass: 8°, 14°, 20°** — the min, mid, and max of
  the requested 8–20° range, to bound the two ends and show angle
  scales linearly. Foot heights (min-foot floor = 10.0mm):
  - LEFT: 8° → 24.8 / 10.0 / 27.2mm; 14° → 36.2 / 10.0 / 40.5mm;
    20° → 48.2 / 10.0 / 54.5mm (at the 3 chosen points, in the same order).
  - RIGHT: 8° → 10.0 / 26.3 / 26.2mm; 14° → 10.0 / 38.9 / 38.7mm;
    20° → 10.0 / 52.1 / 51.9mm.
  - Increment reasoning: 8/14/20° spans the requested range in two even
    6° steps — large enough that each step is a meaningfully different
    typing angle, small enough that the tallest foot stays under ~55mm.
- Rotation is modeled as a pure roll about a Y-parallel (front-to-back)
  axis, height increasing toward the half's inner/thumb-side edge (+X for
  LEFT, −X for RIGHT) — standard "palms-together" tenting direction.

## Battery bay: placement and clearance verification

- **Cell assumption:** protected flat-top 18650, modeled as 18.6mm dia. ×
  65.5mm length (nominal + wrap/cap slop) — confirm against your actual
  cell+holder SKU (open question below).
- **Placement:** LEFT pod center (46.5, 12.6); RIGHT pod center
  (−69.5, 7.0) — both near the inner/thick edge, oriented with the cell's
  long axis along Y, inset 2mm from the plate's true edge.
- **Non-interference with per-key sockets/controller — verified by
  construction, not eyeballing:** the pod (and the feet) are built
  entirely at Z ≤ 0. Every case-side solid has ZMin ≥ 2.63mm (measured
  above). Since `pod.BoundBox.ZMax <= 0` is asserted in the script for
  every generated solid, there is a hard ≥2.63mm Z gap between our new
  parts and _anything_ in the case (including all key-socket solids and
  the sensor-mount cluster) — collision is geometrically impossible
  regardless of XY position. This was the specific numeric check the task
  asked for; it holds by construction rather than a bounding-box
  intersection test in XY (which would be unnecessary here, but the
  script also confirms the pod stays within the plate's XY extent).
- **Worst-case sizing:** pod depth is computed against the _shallowest_
  angle in the family (8°, least ground clearance at a given point) and
  re-checked at generation time — if clearance were insufficient the
  script raises an exception instead of silently emitting bad geometry
  (this happened once during development at an earlier, badly-chosen pod
  location and was caught this way, not by inspection). Final depths:
  LEFT 26.3mm, RIGHT 26.6mm, both ≥ the 22.6mm minimum (18.6mm cell +
  2×2.0mm wall) with ≥1.5mm margin to the table at 8°.
- The nice!nano v2 controller isn't in the STEP model (it's a PCB/holder
  concern, not case geometry) — its Z ≥ 2.63mm case-interior placement
  means it is likewise unaffected by anything at Z ≤ 0. Wire routing from
  the pod up into the case is **not yet resolved** — see open questions.

## FreeCAD CSG results — explicitly verified, not assumed

Ran via `& 'C:\Program Files\FreeCAD 1.1\bin\freecadcmd.exe' 'build_v03.py'`.
For **every** generated solid (3 foot-sets × 2 halves, 1 battery pod ×
2 halves — 8 solids total), the script asserts and prints:

- `shape.isValid()` → **True** in all 8 cases.
- `shape.Volume > 0` → confirmed (fused feet 10.7k–24.6k mm³, pods
  22.2k/22.7k mm³ depending on angle/half).
- `shape.BoundBox.ZMax <= 0.001` → **True** in all 8 cases (proves the
  Z-based collision-avoidance argument above actually holds for the
  as-built geometry, not just in theory).

Exported per half to `hardware/cad/v0.3/<half>/`:

- `<half>_feet_8deg.stl`, `_feet_14deg.stl`, `_feet_20deg.stl` (fused
  3-foot sets, boolean-cut with M3 clearance holes).
- `<half>_battery_pod.stl` (cell bore + 2 mount holes cut into the tray).
- `<half>_v03.FCStd` (native FreeCAD document containing all of the above
  as `Part::Feature` objects, one FCStd per half covering all 3 angles +
  the pod).

## Open questions needing your input

1. **Confirm the target tenting angle** (or angle(s) to keep). 8/14/20°
   were generated as the range's bookends+midpoint — tell me if you want
   a specific single angle, a different increment (e.g. 4° steps), or a
   non-linear set.
2. **Battery wire routing.** The pod sits at Z≤0, entirely below the
   plate; nothing in the current STEP model provides a via/slot through
   the plate for battery leads to reach a PCB above it. Do you want a new
   small routing hole cut through the plate near the pod (would need your
   sign-off since it modifies the "preserve existing plate" assumption),
   or should wires route around the plate's outer edge instead?
3. **Cell + holder SKU.** Pod dimensions used generic 18650 nominal
   (18.6×65.5mm). If you have a specific flat-top protected cell + holder
   part number, its real dimensions may change the pod size/depth margin.
4. **The 8th plate hole per half** (paired with a case boss at Z 6.4–11.4,
   not the Z 3–8 depth used by the other 7) wasn't used for feet or pod
   mounting — do you know what it's for (e.g. thumb-cluster support), and
   should it be preserved/avoided more deliberately?
5. **Foot/pod fastener hardware.** Holes are sized r=1.75mm (M3 clearance,
   matching the plate's own r=1.7mm holes) assuming the existing case
   bosses are usable as-is with longer screws. Confirm screw length/type
   (e.g. M3×20 socket-head) and that the case bosses' internal threads
   (or heat-set inserts) can take a screw arriving from further below.
6. **Print/weight optimization not yet done.** Feet and pod are solid
   blocks (minus their bores), not ribbed/hollowed for print time or
   weight — acceptable for a first fit-check print; flag if you want a
   lightened version before ordering material.
